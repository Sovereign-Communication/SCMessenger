### D1. W1 at current head (NOT FIXED)

The failover re-exchange is NOT genuinely rate-limited. While `allow_failover_reexchange` checks a 3-second cooldown and 5-minute retention window internally, the tracking state (`failover_reexchange_at`) is keyed by `peer_id` and is explicitly cleared by `ledger_exchange_guardrails.forget_peer(&peer_id)` whenever a peer's `num_established` drops to 0 (`swarm.rs:5404`, `:7744`).

**Attack bypass:**
An attacker can bypass the cooldown completely by performing full disconnects:
1. Connect connection A (TCP + Noise).
2. Connect connection B (TCP + Noise). `num_established` = 2.
3. Close connection A. `num_established` drops to 1. `allow_failover_reexchange` triggers a full ledger scan and outbound emission.
4. Close connection B. `num_established` drops to 0. `forget_peer` is called, deleting the cooldown.
5. Repeat.

This restores the exact starvation surface cited in the previous review. The attacker cost is slightly higher (two handshakes per cycle instead of one), but it remains an unrated amplification vector where our node pays the cost of a full ledger scan on demand.

**Required fix:** `failover_reexchange_at` must persist across full disconnects. `forget_peer` must not remove the peer from the cooldown tracking, or the cooldown must be tracked independently.

### D2. F1 at current head (FIXED)

An off-LAN or relayed peer cannot obtain private ledger entries by any path.

1. **Inbound requests:** `exchange_response_entries_for_request` (`swarm.rs:4377`) passes `Some(conn.remote_addr)` to `is_disclosable_on_rfc1918_network`. The filter explicitly rejects `P2pCircuit` for both the requester address and the entry address. Off-LAN connections fail the `same_private_subnet` check.
2. **Outbound requests:** The wrapper `exchange_response_entries` (called at `swarm.rs:5259`, `5360`, `6316`) passes `None` for `requester_addr`. `is_disclosable_on_rfc1918_network` safely fails closed on `None`. This guarantees that no private entries are ever included in outbound `ShareLedger` broadcasts.

### D3. F2-F5 regression check (CLEAN)

* **F4:** Remains fixed. The disclosure filter at `ledger_entry.rs:1181` correctly enforces `e.success_count > 0` and no longer accepts `|| e.public_key.is_some()`. Remote-supplied unproven addresses cannot be redistributed.
* **F5:** Remains fixed. `peer_is_blocked` (`swarm.rs:66`) safely fails closed (`unwrap_or(true)`) if the core handle is missing or if the database lookup returns an error. The transport block gate cannot be bypassed by storage errors.

### D4. `internet.rs` and `manager.rs` PR #165 sanity check (CLEAN)

* `internet.rs`: Adds a `.max(1)` window to prevent division-by-zero during bandwidth calculation for zero-duration connections.
* `manager.rs`: Uses `saturating_sub` to prevent integer underflow during latency scoring.

These are pure mathematical stability fixes and do not interact with the ledger-disclosure or peer-blocking paths.

### D5. Other gated-file deltas (CLEAN)

* `dial_policy.rs`: Switched `relay_addrs` to a `HashSet` to prevent duplicate `p2p-circuit` entries, and strictly preserves transport prefixes. Prevents nested and self-targeted routes.
* `observation.rs`: `ConnectionTracker` now safely stores multiple connections per peer using `connection_id`, directly supporting the F1 fix without clobbering direct/relayed paths.
